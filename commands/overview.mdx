GiveawayX works in servers. Type `/` to see the commands, or use the [prefix](/commands/prefix) (default `g!`). This index covers all slash commands and links to dedicated pages for each category. [Prefix commands](/commands/prefix) and [right-click context menu commands](/commands/context-menus) are documented on their own pages and are not listed here as general commands.

## All commands

**Permissions note:** This column shows the usual Discord permission. Giveaway-management commands also accept a configured Giveaway Manager role (and bot-owner/Admin exceptions where implemented). Some commands with a nominal permission such as “Administrator” additionally check bot-owner/developer identity; a displayed permission alone may not be sufficient.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
