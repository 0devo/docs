GiveawayX works in servers. Type `/` to see the commands, or use the [prefix](/commands/prefix) (default `g!`). This index covers all slash commands and links to dedicated pages for each category. [Prefix commands](/commands/prefix) and [right-click context menu commands](/commands/context-menus) are documented on their own pages and are not listed here as general commands.
