Premium is applied **per server**. A server's tier raises its limits and unlocks extra customization. Servers without Premium use the **Free** tier, which already includes everything you need to run great giveaways.

<Columns cols={4}>
  <Card title="Free" icon="circle">Core giveaways and tools.</Card>
  <Card title="Tier 1 · Basic" icon="1">Customization and reaction entry.</Card>
  <Card title="Tier 2 · Pro" icon="2">Full branding and advanced tools.</Card>
  <Card title="Tier 3 · Enterprise" icon="3">The highest limits.</Card>
</Columns>

## Limits

| | Free | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Longest giveaway | 60 days | 365 days | 730 days | 1095 days |
| Most winners | 60 | 200 | 400 | 1000 |
| Most repeats | 5 | 20 | 40 | 100 |

<Note>
The `repeat` option itself needs at least Tier 1.
</Note>

## What each tier unlocks

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Free">
    <Badge color="gray">Included</Badge>

    - Create giveaways with `/gstart` and `/gcreate`
    - Button entry
    - Basic winner and host notifications
    - Drops, scheduling and templates
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tier 1 · Basic">
    <Badge color="blue">Tier 1</Badge>

    - Everything in Free
    - Custom embed color, labels, messages and button text
    - **Reaction** entry
    - Winner thread name, message and color
    - Custom winner and host DM messages
    - Custom bot nickname for your server
    - Repeating giveaways
    - Bypass roles and advanced bonus-entry options
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tier 2 · Pro">
    <Badge color="purple">Tier 2</Badge>

    - Everything in Tier 1
    - Bot avatar, banner and bio customization
    - Longer giveaways and higher winner and reroll limits
    - **Auto Ping** reminders for active giveaways
    - **Sticky giveaways**
    - Advanced winner role features
    - Multi-Server Add-on
    - Priority support
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tier 3 · Enterprise">
    <Badge color="orange">Tier 3</Badge>

    Everything in Tier 2 with the **highest limits** for duration, winners and repeats, and the biggest leveling allowances.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

`/setcolor` and `/setemoji` are **Premium only**.

## Free trial

Eligible servers can start a **one-time 3-day Tier 1 trial** using the **Start trial** button that appears on Premium prompts. Each server can use the trial once.

## Activate Premium

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subscribe">
    Choose the **Basic**, **Pro** or **Enterprise** tier on Patreon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your server">
    Run `/premium-link` in the server you want to upgrade. You get a link code that works for **30 minutes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or activate instantly">
    `/premium-activate` activates Premium straight from your Patreon status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    `/premium-info` shows your server's tier and expiry date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Each successful payment adds **30 days**. You get reminders **14, 7 and 1 day** before it ends. Subscribers also receive a Premium role in the support server.
</Info>

To move a **Tier 2** subscription to another server, the server owner runs `/premium-transfer`.
