Voting for GiveawayX on bot lists helps the bot grow, and **it rewards you**. You can vote again every **12 hours**.

## Where to vote

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Top.gg" icon="thumbs-up">
    The vote is detected automatically within moments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Discord Bot List" icon="list">
    Also detected automatically, and it adds a bonus **claim** button (see below).
  </Card>
</Columns>

## What you get

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Vote role" icon="user-tag">
    A temporary role in the support server. It is removed automatically **12 hours** after your vote.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bonus giveaway entries" icon="ticket">
    **+1 extra entry** in every active giveaway you have already joined.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Vote reminder" icon="bell">
    A DM telling you when you can vote again.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### How the bonus entries work

- The extra entry applies to giveaways that are **active when you vote and that you have joined**.
- Hosts can cap the bonus with the giveaway's `max_entries` option (1 to 10). 0 means unlimited.
- Vote entries only count while your vote is **current (12 hours)**. After that they are cleaned up automatically.
- The same vote never counts twice, even if the list sends it again.

<Tip>
Vote just before a giveaway you care about ends, since the extra entry is added to giveaways you are already in.
</Tip>

## Discord Bot List claim bonus

Votes from Discord Bot List send you a message with a **claim** button. Pressing it **doubles** your current vote entries in the active giveaways you joined, for a limited time. If you do not press it in time, the button expires.

## Vote reminders

When your 12 hours are up, the bot DMs you a reminder with a link to vote again.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I do not want reminders" icon="bell-slash">
    Press the **opt-out** button on a reminder. The opt-out is permanent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My reminder was late or never came" icon="clock">
    The bot checks for due reminders every minute. A reminder that is **more than a day late is skipped** instead of arriving at a strange time. Closed DMs also block reminders.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to be in the support server?" icon="server">
    The **vote role** is given in the support server, so join it to receive that reward. Bonus giveaway entries work in every server.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
Hosts can see and use vote entries as a [bonus entry source](/giveaways/bonus-entries).
</Note>


## How to use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the bot's page">Go to GiveawayX on **Top.gg** or **Discord Bot List**.</Step>
  <Step title="Press Vote">Sign in with Discord and vote. Do it again every 12 hours.</Step>
  <Step title="Get your rewards">The bot detects the vote within moments. Your vote role is given in the support server, and your active giveaways get +1 entry.</Step>
  <Step title="Claim the bonus (Discord Bot List)">Press the **claim** button in the message the bot sends you before it expires.</Step>
</Steps>
