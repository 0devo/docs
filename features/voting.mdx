Voting for GiveawayX on bot lists supports the bot and **rewards you**. You can vote every **12 hours**.

## What you get

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Vote role" icon="user-tag">
    A temporary role in the support server, removed automatically after 12 hours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bonus entries" icon="ticket">
    **+1 extra entry** in every active giveaway you have joined.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reminders" icon="bell">
    A DM when you can vote again.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
Vote entries count while your vote is current (12 hours). The host can cap how many vote entries one member can earn with the giveaway's `max_entries` option.
</Info>

## Where to vote

The bot supports **Top.gg** and **Discord Bot List**. Discord Bot List votes also give a **claim** button: pressing it **doubles** your vote entries in your active giveaways for a limited time.

## Reminders

Reminders arrive by DM when you are able to vote again. You can **opt out permanently** with a button on the reminder.

<Note>
If a reminder is more than a day late, it is skipped instead of being sent late.
</Note>
