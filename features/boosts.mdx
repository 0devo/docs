GiveawayX keeps count of how many times each member has **boosted your server**, so you can reward boosters in giveaways and thank them on the dashboard.

## How boosts are counted

Whenever someone boosts the server, Discord posts a boost message in the server. The bot reads these messages and adds them to that member's total:

- One boost adds **1**.
- If a member applies several boosts at once, all of them are added.
- The date of their **latest boost** is remembered.

<Warning>
The bot can only count boosts it **sees**. Boosts made before GiveawayX joined the server, or while it could not read the boost messages, are not counted automatically.
</Warning>

## Use boosts in giveaways

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Require boosters" icon="lock" href="/giveaways/entry-requirements">
    Only boosting members can enter, or members who boosted a minimum number of times.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reward boosters" icon="ticket" href="/giveaways/bonus-entries">
    Give boosters extra entries with a role bonus such as `@Booster 3`.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
Pair a booster-only giveaway with a **bonus entry** giveaway for everyone else. Boosters feel valued, and the rest of the community still has a chance.
</Tip>

## Thank-you messages

On the dashboard, the **Boosts** page lets you set up thank-you messages for boosters, including messages sent in the server or by direct message. See [Web Dashboard](/features/dashboard).


## How to use

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Require boosters" icon="lock">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Start the giveaway in the Studio">Run `/gcreate` and choose **Customize in Studio**, or run `/gstart`.</Step>
      <Step title="Open Entry Requirements">Go to **Giveaway Settings**, then **Entry Requirements**.</Step>
      <Step title="Set the boost rules">Use **Boost Count** (minimum boosts given) and **Boost Duration** (days of continuous boosting). 0 turns each one off.</Step>
      <Step title="Publish">Choose **Review and Publish**.</Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Reward boosters" icon="ticket">
    ```text
    /gstart duration:7d winners:1 prize:VIP Role extra_entries:@Booster 3
    ```
    Members with the `@Booster` role get 3 extra entries.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
