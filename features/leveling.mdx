<Warning>
XP tracking needs an **active Premium subscription** (any tier). If Premium ends, tracking stops but your members' XP and your settings are kept. See [Premium](/concepts/premium).
</Warning>

## How members earn XP

| Source | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Chatting | 15 XP (plus or minus 10), once every 60 seconds |
| Voice channels | 10 XP per minute |
| Reactions (optional, off by default) | 5 XP (plus or minus 2), once every 30 seconds |

Text and voice XP are tracked **separately**, so the leaderboard can show Text and Voice side by side. A member's total XP combines both.

You can exclude chosen roles, text channels and voice channels from earning XP. Voice XP can also skip members who are muted or deafened.

## For admins: /levelsetup

`/levelsetup` opens a panel where you control everything.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="XP and cooldowns" icon="gauge">
    Set XP per message, the random variance, the cooldown, XP per voice minute and XP per reaction. The maximum values depend on your Premium tier.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Multipliers and boost events" icon="bolt">
    Give roles or channels an XP multiplier, or run a timed **XP boost event** such as double XP weekend.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Level-up messages" icon="bell">
    Write separate messages for text, voice, reaction and DM level-ups, with optional images. Send them in the channel, by DM, both, or turn them off. You can set a dedicated channel for text and voice level-ups.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Role rewards" icon="award">
    Give roles at specific levels, for text and voice separately. You can stack rewards, remove the previous reward, or keep only the highest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Streaks" icon="fire">
    Reward daily activity with extra XP per streak day (2 percent by default) up to a cap (100 percent by default).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Leaderboard timing" icon="calendar">
    Choose your time zone and when the daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards reset. Leaderboards can be **fixed** (start of the week or month) or **rolling** (the last N days).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Admin commands

<ParamField body="/setxp" type="command">Set a member's XP. Choose `total`, `text` or `voice`.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="/setlevel" type="command">Set a member's level (0 to 999). Choose `total`, `text` or `voice`, and whether to keep their XP.</ParamField>
