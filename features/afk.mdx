---
title: "AFK status"
description: "Tell everyone you are away, and get a tidy summary of who mentioned you when you return."
icon: "moon"
---

AFK ("away from keyboard") lets you tell the server you are away. While you are AFK, anyone who mentions you is told **why** and **for how long**. When you come back, the bot gives you a **summary of every mention you missed** with links to jump straight to each message.

## Set your status

<ParamField body="reason" type="text">Why you are away, up to **200 characters**. If you leave it empty the reason is simply "AFK".</ParamField>
<ParamField body="global" type="true / false">Make your AFK status work in **every server** you share with the bot, not only this one. Default: false.</ParamField>

<CodeGroup>
```text Simple
/afk
```
```text With a reason
/afk reason:Having dinner, back at 8 PM
```
```text Everywhere
/afk reason:Travelling global:true
```
</CodeGroup>

The bot confirms with a **private message** that only you can see.

<Warning>
Your reason **cannot contain links, Discord invites or mentions** (including `@everyone` and `@here`). This stops AFK from being used to spam or ping people. If it does, the bot asks you to remove them and try again.
</Warning>

## Local and global AFK

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Local (default)" icon="server">
    Active only in the server where you ran the command.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Global" icon="globe">
    Active in **every shared server** at the moment you turn it on. The confirmation tells you how many servers it covers. Messages marked global show a 🌍.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Note>
A server only ever sees **its own** AFK entry. Your reason and mentions in one server are never revealed in another.
</Note>

## What happens when someone mentions you

<Steps>
  <Step title="The bot replies">
    It shows that you are AFK, the **reason**, and **how long ago** you went AFK.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Several AFK members in one message">
    If someone mentions more than one AFK member, all of them are shown in **one single reply**, separated by dividers, instead of one reply each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The mention is saved for you">
    The bot remembers who mentioned you, in which channel, a link to the message, and the first part of what they wrote.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The bot stays quiet when:**

- the mentioned user is a **bot**;
- you **mention yourself**;
- the same person mentioned the same AFK member **less than a minute ago** (so repeated pings do not trigger repeated replies);
- the AFK member has just returned.

## What happens when you come back

Send **any message** in a server where you are AFK. Your status is removed automatically, and the bot replies with:

- a **Welcome back** message that shows **how long you were AFK**;
- the **number of mentions** you received;
- the **latest 10 mentions**, each with the sender, how long ago it happened, and a **Jump to Message** button;
- a line such as "and 3 more" if there were more than 10.

For a **global** AFK, speaking in any server removes your status **everywhere**, and the summary includes the server name for each mention.

<Tip>
Pair a clear reason with a time, like `Back at 8 PM`, so people know when to expect a reply.
</Tip>

## For admins

AFK can be switched off for your server from the dashboard's command settings, and its use can be limited to certain channels or roles. See [Command customization](/features/command-customization).
