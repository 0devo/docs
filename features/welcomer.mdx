The Welcomer sends a message when someone **joins** or **leaves** your server. You design everything on the **dashboard** (Welcome and Goodbye page) with a live preview. Joining and leaving are configured separately, so you can have a warm welcome and a short goodbye.

## What happens on a join or leave

<Steps>
  <Step title="Someone joins or leaves">
    The bot reacts straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Placeholders are filled in">
    Your message is personalized with the member's details (see the table below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The message is sent">
    To your chosen channel, to the member by **DM**, or both. If a DM cannot be delivered, the bot can fall back to the channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cleanup and roles">
    The message can **delete itself** after a set time, and auto roles are given to new members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Message options

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Enabled and channel** | Turn the welcome (or goodbye) on and pick where it goes. |
| **Format** | A plain **text** message or an **embed**. |
| **DM the member** | Send privately. Optionally **fall back to the channel** when DMs are closed. |
| **Mention the member** | Ping them in the channel message. |
| **Delete after** | Remove the message after 0 to 3600 seconds (0 keeps it). |
| **Ignore bots** | Skip the message when the new member is a bot. On by default. |

## Placeholders

Type these in any text field. They are replaced for every member.

| Placeholder | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `{user}` | A mention of the member |
| `{username}` | Their username |
| `{server}` | Your server's name |
| `{memberCount}` | The current member count |
| `{avatar}` | The member's avatar picture |
| `{serverIcon}` | Your server's icon |
| `{accountAge}` | How old their Discord account is, like "2 years" or "5 days" |
| `{userCreatedDate}` | The date the account was created |
| `{timeInServer}` | How long they have been in the server (useful in goodbye messages) |

<CodeGroup>
```text Welcome
Welcome {user} to {server}! You are member #{memberCount}.
```
```text Goodbye
{username} has left after {timeInServer}. We'll miss you!
```
</CodeGroup>

<Note>
`{avatar}` and `{serverIcon}` also work inside image and thumbnail fields of an embed.
</Note>

## Embeds

An embed welcome supports a **color**, author, title, description, thumbnail, image, footer, up to **10 fields** and a timestamp. If you do not pick a color, joins use **green** and leaves use **red**.

## Image cards

The bot can generate a personalized **image card** with the member's avatar and attach it to the message.

<Columns cols={4}>
  <Card title="Classic" icon="image" />
  <Card title="Banner" icon="panorama" />
  <Card title="Minimal" icon="minus" />
  <Card title="Frame" icon="border-all" />
</Columns>

You can choose a background color or image, the overlay opacity, an accent color, the title and subtitle text, and the avatar's **shape** (circle, square or hexagon), size and border.

## Auto roles

Give new members roles the moment they join, with **separate lists for humans and for bots**.

<Warning>
For safety the bot **never assigns a role that has Administrator permission** through auto roles, even if you add one. Choose ordinary roles.
</Warning>

## Raid protection

Set a **minimum account age** (0 to 365 days). Accounts younger than that are treated as suspicious. Turn on **skip on gate** to send **no welcome message at all** for those accounts, so raiders do not get a greeting.

<Info>
With **Ignore bots** on, bot accounts get no message, but they **still receive the bot auto roles** you set.
</Info>

<Tip>
Use the **test message** button on the dashboard to preview a welcome or goodbye in your channel before going live.
</Tip>
