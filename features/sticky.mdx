A **sticky message** is re-posted as people chat so it stays visible. Perfect for rules, announcements or "read this first" notes. Manage them with `/sticky` (requires **Manage Messages**).

## Building a sticky

Sticky messages are built from blocks, up to **12 blocks** each:

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Text" icon="font">Formatted text.</Card>
  <Card title="Separator" icon="minus">A divider line.</Card>
  <Card title="Section" icon="table-columns">Text with an accessory.</Card>
  <Card title="Image" icon="image">A picture or gallery.</Card>
  <Card title="Button" icon="rectangle-ad">A clickable button.</Card>
</Columns>

## When it is re-sent

| Setting | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Every message | After each new message (default) |
| Every 5 or 10 messages | After that many messages |
| Every 30 seconds | At most once per 30 seconds |
| Every minute | At most once per minute |

Anything other than "every message" needs <Badge color="blue">Tier 1</Badge>.

## Timing options

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Detection">
    **Immediate**, or **delayed**, which waits for a pause in the conversation (5 seconds by default) before re-posting so it does not interrupt a fast chat.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cleanup">
    Choose when the old copy is deleted: when the new one starts, when it ends (default), or after a delay (5 seconds by default).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Triggers

Depending on your tier, a sticky can also appear on a **keyword**, on a **schedule**, up to a **maximum number of times**, with a **per-user cooldown**, or when someone **reacts** to a message.
