</Info>

With temporary voice channels, members don't need a moderator to get a voice room. They join a special **hub channel**, and the bot instantly creates a new voice channel just for them and moves them into it.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A member joins the hub">
    The hub is a normal voice channel that an admin marked as a hub.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A new channel is created">
    The bot creates a voice channel named after the member, in the hub's category, and moves them in automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The member owns it">
    The owner can manage the channel: rename it, set a user limit, mute or deafen others, and more. See the controls below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It cleans itself up">
    When everyone has left, the channel is **deleted automatically**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Naming

The channel name comes from a pattern. These placeholders are replaced for each member:

| Placeholder | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `[displayName]` | The member's server display name |
| `[userName]` | The member's username |
| `[userId]` | The member's ID |

The default pattern shows the owner's name followed by "Channel". Names are limited to 100 characters.

## Rules and defaults

| Rule | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Channels per member | 1 by default; a hub can allow up to 5. |
| Already at your limit? | Joining the hub again moves you back to your existing channel instead of creating another. |
| User limit | Default **unlimited** (0). |
| Audio quality (bitrate) | Default 64 kbps, never above what your server's boost level allows: **96 kbps** (no boosts), **128 kbps** (level 1), **256 kbps** (level 2) or **384 kbps** (level 3). |
| Video quality | Auto, or Full. |
| Owner leaves | If **auto transfer** is on and others remain, ownership passes to someone still in the channel. Otherwise the owner simply leaves it. |

<Note>
While a member owns a channel from a hub, the hub is locked for them, so they can't spam new channels. It unlocks when their channel is deleted or they hand it over.
</Note>

## What the owner can control

Each control can be switched on or off by the admin for a hub.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Channel" icon="sliders">
    Rename, user limit, bitrate, region, slowmode, voice status, video quality, NSFW (off by default).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Privacy" icon="lock">
    Lock the channel, hide it, invite someone (creates an invite and DMs them).
  </Card>
  <Card title="People" icon="users">
    Kick, ban, mute and deafen members in the channel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ownership" icon="crown">
    Transfer the channel to another member, or **claim** it when the owner is gone.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## What the bot needs

The bot must be able to **Manage Channels**, **Move Members**, **Mute Members**, **Deafen Members**, **View Channel** and **Connect** in the hub's category.

<Warning>
If the bot can't create the channel or move the member, nothing is left behind: a half-created channel is deleted straight away.
</Warning>
