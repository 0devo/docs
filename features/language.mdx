Run `/setlang` to change the bot's language for your whole server. Everything the bot says, from giveaway messages to command replies, switches to that language.

## Time zone

Your server's time zone starts as **UTC** and is used for features such as leaderboard resets. Leveling has its own time zone setting.

<Tip>
You can also confirm the language the first time the bot joins, from the **Getting Started** message.
</Tip>
