GiveawayX can post a **log message** whenever something happens, so your team always knows who did what.

## What can be logged

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Giveaway actions" icon="gift">
    Start, end, edit, delete, reroll, pause, resume, drop, last-to-type, fix and schedule.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Settings changes" icon="gear">
    Color, emoji, role, DM, winner thread, language and log settings changes, and command picks.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Each event type can be switched on or off. Logs can go to one or more channels, or to **different channels per event or group**.
