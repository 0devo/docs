---
title: "Welcome and goodbye placeholders"
description: "Variables for join and leave messages, embeds and image cards."
icon: "hand-wave"
---

Edit welcome and goodbye messages on the dashboard. See [Welcomer](/features/welcomer).

| Placeholder | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `{user}` | A mention of the member |
| `{username}` | Their username |
| `{server}` | Your server's name |
| `{memberCount}` | The current member count |
| `{avatar}` | The member's avatar picture |
| `{serverIcon}` | Your server's icon |
| `{accountAge}` | How old their Discord account is, like "2 years" |
| `{userCreatedDate}` | The date the account was created |
| `{timeInServer}` | How long they were in the server. Best for goodbye messages |

<CodeGroup>
```text Welcome
Welcome {user} to {server}! You are member #{memberCount}.
```
```text Goodbye
{username} left after {timeInServer}. We'll miss you!
```
</CodeGroup>

<Note>
`{avatar}` and `{serverIcon}` also work in the **image** and **thumbnail** fields of an embed.
</Note>
