---
title: "Level-up message placeholders"
description: "Variables for text, voice, reaction and DM level-up messages."
icon: "chart-line"
---

Level-up messages are written in `/levelsetup` (see [Leveling](/features/leveling)). You can write a separate message for **text**, **voice**, **reaction** and **DM** level-ups, up to 500 characters each.

| Placeholder | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `{user}` | A mention of the member |
| `{userName}` | The member's name without a mention |
| `{level}` | The level they just reached |
| `{server}` | Your server's name |

<CodeGroup>
```text Text
GG {user}! Level {level}!
```
```text Voice
Congrats {user}! Voice Level {level}!
```
```text Reaction
Congrats {user}, reaction level {level}!
```
```text DM
Hey {user}, you leveled up in {server}!
```
</CodeGroup>

<Tip>
Use `{userName}` in places where a mention would ping the member, such as an embed title.
</Tip>
