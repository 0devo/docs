## /gdrop: first to claim wins

A **drop** has no draw. The first members to claim the prize win **immediately**. Perfect for surprise rewards.

<ParamField body="prize" type="text" required>What is being given away.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="winners" type="number (1 to 50)">How many people can claim. Default 1.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="duration" type="text">How long the drop runs, like `30m`.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="channel" type="channel">Where to host the drop.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="image" type="file">Main image (images only).</ParamField>
<ParamField body="thumbnail" type="file">Small image (images only).</ParamField>
<ParamField body="countdown" type="number (1 to 30)">Seconds of countdown before the drop appears, so everyone can get ready.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="blacklist_role" type="role">A role that cannot claim.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="required_role" type="role">A role needed to claim.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="message" type="text">An extra message sent before the drop.</ParamField>

<Note>
Drops cannot be rerolled. The bot also checks its permissions in the channel first and tells you what is missing.
</Note>

## /glast: first or last to type wins

A quick chat game. Members type in a channel, and the **first** or the **last** person to type wins.

<ParamField body="channel" type="channel" required>The text channel for the game.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="duration" type="text" required>1 to 10 minutes, like `5m` or `300s`.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="prize" type="text" required>The prize.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="mode" type="choice" required>**First to type wins** or **Last to type wins**.</ParamField>

Requires **Manage Server** or **Administrator**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="First to type">
    A race. Fast fingers win. Use a short duration.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Last to type">
    A waiting game. The winner is whoever types last before time runs out, so people keep typing to stay in. It gets chaotic fast.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>


## How to use

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Start a drop" icon="bolt">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Run /gdrop">Type `/gdrop` and enter the **prize** (the only required option).</Step>
      <Step title="Choose how many can win">Set `winners` (1 to 50). Add a `countdown` of 1 to 30 seconds so members can get ready.</Step>
      <Step title="Send it">The drop appears in the channel. The first members to press **claim** win straight away.</Step>
    </Steps>

    <CodeGroup>
    ```text Simplest
    /gdrop prize:$10 Gift Card
    ```
    ```text Fuller
    /gdrop prize:$10 Gift Card winners:3 countdown:10 duration:30m channel:#drops required_role:@Member
    ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Start a first or last to type game" icon="keyboard">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Run /glast">Fill in all four options: `channel`, `duration`, `prize` and `mode`.</Step>
      <Step title="Pick the mode">Choose **First to type wins** or **Last to type wins**.</Step>
      <Step title="Send it">Members type in the channel until the time is up. The bot announces the winner.</Step>
    </Steps>

    ```text
    /glast channel:#general duration:5m prize:Discord Nitro mode:First to type wins
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
