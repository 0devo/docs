This page is for **members**. If you host giveaways, see [Entry requirements](/giveaways/entry-requirements) to control who qualifies.

## How to enter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Enter">
    Press the **Enter** button on the giveaway message. If the giveaway uses reaction entry, react with the emoji shown instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pass any checks">
    The bot checks the giveaway's requirements. Some giveaways also ask you to solve a **captcha** or upload a **screenshot** as proof (see below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your confirmation">
    You receive a **private message** that confirms your entry. It also shows any **bonus entries** you earned from your roles, and may suggest ways to earn more, such as voting for the bot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Entering never posts anything public. All confirmations and error messages are visible only to you.
</Tip>

## If you are asked for a captcha or a screenshot

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Captcha" icon="robot">
    A form shows a short random code. Type it **exactly** as shown and submit. The code is 4 digits on easy, 5 characters on medium and 7 on hard, and it expires after 5 minutes. Your entry counts only after you pass.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Screenshot proof" icon="camera">
    For giveaways that ask you to follow, subscribe, like or comment somewhere, press the **verification** button and upload one screenshot that shows you did it. An AI checks it. You get **3 attempts** per giveaway, with a short wait of about 30 seconds between tries. Once you pass, press **Enter** again to join; you are not asked to verify twice.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Why was my entry denied?

If you do not qualify, the bot tells you privately which requirement you missed.

| Reason | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Required role** | You do not have the role (or enough of the roles) the giveaway needs. | Get the role, or ask a moderator. |
| **Blacklisted role** | One of your roles is blocked from this giveaway. | Nothing; this is set by the host. |
| **Server boost count** | You have not boosted the server enough times. | Boost the server. |
| **Boost duration** | You have not been boosting long enough. | Keep boosting; the time counts up. |
| **Profile completeness** | Your Discord profile is missing a custom avatar or banner or accent color. | Complete your profile. |
| **Username check** | Your name looks like a throw-away or bot account. | Change your name if it is a false alarm. |
| **Messages** | You have not sent enough messages. | Chat more in the server. |
| **Server join age** | You have not been in the server long enough. | Wait, then try again. |
| **Account age** | Your Discord account is too new. | Wait, then try again. |
| **Official server tag** | You are not displaying the server's tag. | Equip the tag on your profile. |
| **Level** | Your level is too low. | Earn XP. See [Leveling](/features/leveling). |
| **Verification** | You have not passed the captcha or screenshot check. | Complete it. |
| **Winner cooldown** | You won recently and the server pauses winners for a while. | Wait for the cooldown to end. |

## Leaving a giveaway

Changed your mind? Press **Enter** again. Because you are already in, the bot shows an **Already participating** message with a **Leave Giveaway** button. Press it to remove your entry.

<Note>
You can only leave while the giveaway is active. If it already ended, or you were not entered, the bot tells you so.
</Note>

<Warning>
**Boost giveaways:** if a giveaway requires boosting and you stop boosting while it is still running, you are **automatically removed** from it. Members with a bypass role for boosts stay in.
</Warning>

## Giveaway streaks

Some giveaways give **bonus entries for streaks**. Your streak is the number of giveaways in a row that you took part in **in that server**.

- Take part in a giveaway and your streak goes up by 1 when it finishes.
- Miss a giveaway and your streak starts again from 1 the next time you enter.

See [Bonus entries](/giveaways/bonus-entries) for how hosts turn streaks into extra chances.

## See who else entered

Press the **participants** button on the giveaway message, if the host left it enabled. See [Participants panel](/giveaways/participants-panel).
