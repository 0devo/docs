## When a giveaway ends

The bot draws winners from the entries (weighted by [bonus entries](/giveaways/bonus-entries)) and updates the giveaway message. Then, depending on your settings, it can:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="DM the winners and host" icon="envelope">Private notifications, switchable in `/settings`.</Card>
  <Card title="Open a winner thread" icon="comments">A place for winners to claim and talk to the host.</Card>
  <Card title="Give winner roles" icon="user-tag">Automatically, with optional delay and duration.</Card>
  <Card title="Start auto-claim" icon="hand-pointer">One-button claiming with automatic rerolls.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Winner DMs

Winner and host DMs can be switched on or off separately, and there is also a **global DM** switch. With <Badge color="blue">Tier 1</Badge> you can write your own DM messages. A single giveaway can also override the DM message.

## Winner threads

When enabled, the bot creates a thread for the winners after the giveaway.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Auto thread | Turns thread creation on or off. |
| Visibility | **Public** or **Private** thread. |
| Auto-close | How long until the thread closes. |
| Claim tracker | Tracks who has claimed (on by default). |
| Ping winners | Mentions the winners in the thread. |
| Claim deadline | How long winners have to claim. |
| Name, message and color | Customize the thread. <Badge color="blue">Tier 1</Badge> |

More thread options:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Auto-archive | Archives an idle thread after 1 hour, 1 day, 3 days or 7 days (1 day by default). |
| Invite winners | In private threads, lets members who are not moderators add people. On by default. |
| Staff roles | Roles automatically added to every winner thread, up to 50 members per thread (25 by default). |
| Close when complete | Closes the thread once every winner has claimed. Off by default. |

A single giveaway can override the thread settings.

## Winner roles

Give winners a role automatically. Each **winner role rule** can set:

| Option | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Role | The role to give. |
| Reward | Nothing extra, bonus **XP**, or bonus **levels**. |
| Delay | Give it after some minutes, or at a set time. |
| Duration | Permanent, or removed after some minutes. |
| Rotate | Remove the role from earlier winners so only the newest hold it. |

Basic winner roles are free. Advanced options need <Badge color="purple">Tier 2</Badge>.

## Auto-claim

Turn on `auto_claim` and winners claim their prize with **one button**. If a winner does not claim in time, the bot automatically **rerolls** their place.

| Setting | Default | Allowed range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claim window | 24 hours | 60 seconds to 30 days |
| Maximum rerolls | Unlimited | 0 or more |

## Rerolling with /greroll

`/greroll` redraws winners from an **ended** giveaway. It shows a **preview** of exactly what will change before anything happens.

<ParamField body="giveaway_id" type="text">Pick the giveaway from the list, or paste a message ID or message link.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="replace" type="user">Replace **only this winner**, for example someone who never claimed. Everyone else keeps their win.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="count" type="number">Draw this many new winners. This **replaces the whole winner list**.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="exclude_previous_winners" type="true / false">Never pick someone who already won. On by default.</ParamField>

<Warning>
You cannot reroll a giveaway that is still running, paused or scheduled, or a drop. If nobody eligible is left, the reroll is refused and the bot explains why.
</Warning>

The bot remembers how many times a giveaway was rerolled and who did it, which also shows in the giveaway log.

## Winner cooldown

Your server can set a **winner cooldown** in minutes (off by default) so recent winners cannot win again too soon. Each giveaway can follow the server, turn it off, or use its own number of minutes.


## How to use

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Turn on auto-claim" icon="hand-pointer">
    ```text
    /gstart duration:3d winners:2 prize:Nitro auto_claim:true
    ```
    Or open the **Studio**, go to **Giveaway Settings**, then **Advanced**, then **Auto Claim and Reroll** to set the claim deadline and the maximum rerolls.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Reroll a giveaway" icon="rotate">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Run /greroll">Give the giveaway's ID or link. A **preview** of the change appears.</Step>
      <Step title="Choose the kind of reroll">Use `replace` for one person, or `count` for a whole new list.</Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">The new winners are announced and notified.</Step>
    </Steps>

    <CodeGroup>
    ```text Replace one winner
    /greroll giveaway_id:123456789012345678 replace:@Winner
    ```
    ```text Draw 2 new winners
    /greroll giveaway_id:123456789012345678 count:2 exclude_previous_winners:true
    ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Set DMs and threads" icon="gear">
    Run `/settings` and open **DMs and templates** or **Winner thread**. See [Server settings](/giveaways/settings).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
