This page summarizes the release announcements provided by the GiveawayX team. Some older details may have changed; use the linked guides for current instructions.

## Version 3.11 — September 19, 2026

- **Fair winner selection:** Recheck winners against requirements, skip disqualified winners, continue selecting, and remove entries when members stop qualifying. Eligible members may enter again after meeting the requirements.
- **Anti-raid and verification:** Profile-completeness checks, configurable suspicious-username detection, and code-based verification with Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty.
- **Host controls and Server Tag:** Choose and customize the displayed host, its messages and DMs; optionally require members to display the server's official Discord Server Tag.
- **Extra entries:** Multiple rules based on roles, levels, XP, votes, users, Server Tags, or giveaway streaks. Rules support fixed or random amounts, multipliers, per-rule/global caps, scheduled activation, and Stack or Priority mode.
- **Participant management:** Show member position, entry counts, account details, server tenure, and Discord ID; search and page through the list, remove participants, or edit their extra entries.
- **Auto Ping:** Send recurring reminders with selected role, `@everyone`, or `@here` mentions, with configurable interval and limit.

Guides: [requirements](/giveaways/entry-requirements), [extra entries](/giveaways/bonus-entries), [managing giveaways](/giveaways/managing), [Auto Ping](/giveaways/auto-ping).

## Version 3.10 — July 13, 2026

Added `/commands`, a per-server command customization panel for custom replies and variables, aliases, channel/role allow and deny lists, message cleanup, and hiding or disabling commands. Added category navigation and pagination; admins and owners bypass channel and role restrictions.

Guides: [command customization](/features/command-customization), [prefix commands](/commands/prefix).

## Version 3.9 — July 9, 2026

- Unified `/gstart`, `/gedit`, and `/gschedule` with a shared visual editor, live preview, drafts and templates; save changed values and synchronize giveaway messages.
- Rebuilt scheduling with daily/weekly/monthly recurrence, optional role reminders, editable start times, and a schedule manager.
- Rebuilt templates with previews, duplication, import/export, image support, confirmations, and direct start/schedule integration.
- Added Any/All/minimum required-role modes and role-tenure requirements; configurable bypass-role evaluation; and Any/All/minimum/maximum blacklist modes.
- Rebuilt winner-role rewards (instant, delayed, scheduled, permanent, temporary, rotating, XP and levels). Level rewards persist as XP.
- Rebuilt Starboard configuration, filters, restrictions, display builder, rewards, leaderboards, statistics, preview, and import/export.
- Fixed settings preservation, editor consistency, template validation/import, Starboard reactions/navigation, bonus-entry controls, and large slash-command registration; improved maintainability and stability.

Guides: [scheduling](/giveaways/scheduling), [templates](/giveaways/templates), [requirements](/giveaways/entry-requirements), [winner roles](/giveaways/winners-and-claims), [leveling](/features/leveling), [Starboard](/features/starboard).

## Version 3.7 — July 1, 2026

Launched a full XP/leveling system for text, voice, and reactions, with `/rank`, `/top`, `/levelsetup`, `/setlevel`, and `/setxp`; rewards, multipliers, exclusions, and temporary boosts. Redesigned the participants panel; added a `/gstart` confirmation preview; rebuilt `/gschedule` with templates and preview; and added a `/setup` wizard. Fixed message-count progress lost on restart, improved `/settings` navigation, and removed outdated features.

Guides: [leveling](/features/leveling), [managing giveaways](/giveaways/managing), [scheduling](/giveaways/scheduling), [settings](/giveaways/settings).
