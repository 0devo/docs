---
title: "How to stop alt accounts from winning"
description: "Block brand-new accounts, require the captcha and keep giveaways fair."
icon: "shield-check"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set minimum ages">
    Require a minimum **account age** and **server join age**. Brand-new accounts and just-joined alts are blocked.

    ```text
    /gstart duration:3d winners:1 prize:Gift Card account_age:30 joined_age:7
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the captcha">
    In the **Studio** open **Giveaway Settings**, then **Appearance**, then **Entry Verification**, and set **Require captcha** to always on. Pick the difficulty you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add anti-raid checks">
    In **Appearance**, open **Anti-Raid Protection**. Set a **minimum profile completeness** and **suspicious username detection** to low, medium or high.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Require a role earned over time">
    Add a required role together with a **role hold duration**, so a role grabbed just to enter does not count.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
No single check is perfect. Combining account age, the captcha and a hold duration works much better than any one of them.
</Tip>

See [Entry requirements](/giveaways/entry-requirements) for every option.
