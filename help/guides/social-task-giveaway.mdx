---
title: "How to run a \"follow us\" giveaway"
description: "Ask members to prove they followed, subscribed, liked or commented, using a screenshot check."
icon: "share-nodes"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Studio">Run `/gcreate` and choose **Customize in Studio**.</Step>
  <Step title="Turn on screenshot verification">In the entry settings choose the **platform** (YouTube, Kick, X, Instagram, TikTok or Twitch) and the **action** (subscribe, follow, like or comment).</Step>
  <Step title="Give the account name">Enter the channel or account name that must appear in the screenshot. You can add an example screenshot.</Step>
  <Step title="Publish">Choose **Review and Publish**.</Step>
</Steps>

Members press the **verification** button, upload one screenshot, and an AI checks it. They get **3 attempts** per giveaway.

<Note>
This feature may not be enabled on every setup. If you do not see it, ask in the [support server](https://discord.gg/86jArY28hj).
</Note>

See [Entry requirements](/giveaways/entry-requirements#screenshot-verification-for-social-tasks).
