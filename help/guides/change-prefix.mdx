---
title: "How to change the bot prefix"
description: "Use a prefix other than g! for text commands."
icon: "exclamation"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">Sign in at [giveawayx.lol](https://giveawayx.lol) and choose your server.</Step>
  <Step title="Open General Settings">Your command prefixes are listed there, along with the bot language and server time zone.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the prefixes">Add, change or remove prefixes. A server can have several.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">The new prefix works straight away.</Step>
</Steps>

Not sure what a prefix is? See [Prefix commands](/commands/prefix).
