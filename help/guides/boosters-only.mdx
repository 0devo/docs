---
title: "How to run a giveaway for boosters only"
description: "Let only members who boost your server enter, or reward them with extra entries."
icon: "rocket"
---

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Boosters only" icon="lock">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Studio">Run `/gcreate` and choose **Customize in Studio**.</Step>
      <Step title="Set the boost rules">Go to **Giveaway Settings**, then **Entry Requirements**. Set **Boost Count** to 1 or more. Optionally set **Boost Duration** in days.</Step>
      <Step title="Publish">Choose **Review and Publish**.</Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Extra entries for boosters" icon="ticket">
    ```text
    /gstart duration:7d winners:1 prize:VIP Role extra_entries:@Booster 3
    ```
    Everyone can enter, and boosters get 3 extra entries.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
If a member stops boosting while a boosters-only giveaway is running, they are removed from it automatically.
</Warning>

See [Server boosts](/features/boosts).
