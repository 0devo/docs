---
title: "How to give winners a role automatically"
description: "Hand out a role, bonus XP or levels to every winner."
icon: "user-tag"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the role on the command">
    ```text
    /gstart duration:3d winners:3 prize:Nitro winners_roles:@Winner
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or use the Studio for more control">
    Open **Giveaway Settings**, then **Rewards and Bonus Entries**, then **Winner Roles**. Add a delay, a temporary duration, XP or level rewards, or remove the role from previous winners.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the bot's role position">
    The bot's role must be **above** the role it gives, and it needs **Manage Roles**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Advanced winner role options need <Badge color="purple">Tier 2</Badge>. See [Winners and claims](/giveaways/winners-and-claims).
