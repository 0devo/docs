---
title: "A giveaway is stuck or did not end"
description: "Diagnose and repair it with /gfix."
icon: "wrench"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look first">Run `/gfix giveaway_id:<id> dry_run:true` to see a report without changing anything.</Step>
  <Step title="Read the findings">Each one is marked critical, warning or info, with a repair type: automatic, needs confirmation or manual.</Step>
  <Step title="Repair">Run `/gfix giveaway_id:<id>` and confirm the repairs it suggests.</Step>
  <Step title="If the message was deleted">`/gfix` reports it. Use `/gdelete` to remove the giveaway.</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The bot checks for due giveaways every 15 seconds, so a giveaway that is more than a minute late is worth repairing.
</Note>

See [Managing giveaways](/giveaways/managing#gfix-diagnose-and-repair).
