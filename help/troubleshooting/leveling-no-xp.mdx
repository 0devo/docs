---
title: "Leveling gives no XP"
description: "Why members do not level up."
icon: "chart-line"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Premium">XP tracking needs an active Premium subscription on the server. See [Premium](/concepts/premium).</Step>
  <Step title="Check exclusions">The member's role, or the channel, may be excluded from earning XP in `/levelsetup`.</Step>
  <Step title="Check cooldowns">XP is awarded at most once per cooldown (60 seconds by default), so fast chatting does not earn more.</Step>
  <Step title="Voice XP">Voice XP can skip muted or deafened members if that option is on.</Step>
</Steps>
