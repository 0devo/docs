---
title: "A member cannot enter a giveaway"
description: "Find which requirement is stopping them."
icon: "user-xmark"
---

The bot tells the member privately which requirement they missed. Common reasons:

| Reason | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Missing role | Give the role, or add a bypass role |
| Account or server too new | Wait, or lower the minimum age |
| Not enough messages or level | Chat more, or lower the requirement |
| Not boosting | Boost the server, or remove the boost requirement |
| Captcha or screenshot not passed | Complete the check |
| Recent winner cooldown | Wait for the cooldown to end |

See [Entering a giveaway](/giveaways/participating#why-was-my-entry-denied) for the full list.
